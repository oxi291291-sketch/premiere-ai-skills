# Placing SFX and music against picture

Use this reference when the task calls for SFX on a cut, reveal, graphic entrance, camera move, or music-led passage. Use [music.md](music.md) for selection and the review mix, and [finishing-layout.md](finishing-layout.md) for Premiere track ownership.

## Status and scope

**(default), machine-analysed.** The table and timings come from Earshot, waveform and frame-strip analysis of nine short, motion-led Instagram Reels Oxi supplied on 2026-11-13. The cues were never confirmed by ear, the Reels had no stems, and the only test replayed the same nine Reels; the method has not run on one of Oxi's timelines. Present every choice made from this file to Oxi as a proposal to audition. A row becomes a rule when Oxi approves it by ear on a real job: label it `(Oxi, <date>, <job>)` then.

**Oxi's own practice sets the density.** His videos are mostly long and dialogue-led, and what he does there is already recorded: the "Music and SFX" section of the job's profile in [video-profiles/](video-profiles/README.md), the selective-accent and protected-interval rules in [music.md](music.md), and the transition swoosh on topic changes in [finishing-layout.md](finishing-layout.md). Read those first and keep their SFX count and placement. Use the table below to pick the sound family and its anchor for the cues those sources allow. Reel-style density, a cue every second or two, belongs to a short motion-led piece or a passage Oxi asks to treat that way.

## Spot the cue

1. Identify what leads each passage: dialogue, a visual action, a music phrase, or the scene's ambience. Watch the picture from lead-in to settle. Mark the cut, peak blur or speed, first readable frame, and landing separately when they diverge. For source audio such as a product test, apply the protected intervals in [music.md](music.md).
2. Build the cue sheet before choosing files. For each material cue, record the frame or time, the visual anchor and why it matters, the proposed family of sound, and any competing word or beat. Mark picture events that can ride the existing mix. Use a single cue for a cluster of related changes when it carries the whole gesture.
3. Place by the audible part of the file. If the file's anchor is at offset `a` from its source in-point and the picture target is `t`, place the clip at `t - a`. Record onset, anchor, end, and offset to picture. Keep the cue tail through the settling motion when it supports the next shot. Shorten the choice or fade the tail if it masks an upcoming word or action.

## Chọn SFX theo chuyển động và mục đích của hình

Xem đoạn hình từ trước khi chuyển động đến lúc ổn định. Ghi một câu về điều cần làm rõ cho người xem, chẳng hạn “đọc được con số”, “cảm thấy camera lao qua cửa” hoặc “hiểu vật đã chạm mặt bàn”. Chọn chất tiếng và mốc đặt từ câu đó. Nếu khi tắt SFX mà hành động vẫn rõ và nhạc hoặc lời đã làm đúng việc, giữ mix hiện có. Các từ tiếng Anh trong bảng dùng để tìm ứng viên trong [OXI Assets](assets.md). Ví dụ Reel là chứng cứ về cách tiếng đi với hình. Lựa chọn trong cột SFX là phương án thử cho video mới.

| **Lia gấp qua vùng nhòe hoặc vật che ống kính** | Tiếng lướt gió gọn, đuôi ngắn (`short air whoosh`, `whip pan swoosh`). Cho tiếng lên theo đà máy. Canh phần rõ nhất vào pha nhòe hoặc lúc vượt mép vật che, tùy điều người xem cần cảm nhận. | **Camera lao qua cửa, qua vật thể hoặc qua nhiều lớp 3D** | Tiếng gió lướt có thân và độ dài theo hành trình (`air pass-by`, `airy flyby`). Cảm giác đi qua đến từ lúc âm tiến gần, đạt đỉnh rồi rút. Đặt đỉnh ở lúc camera xuyên mép hoặc lộ không gian, tùy điểm muốn nhấn. Barsolmedia khoảng 13,09 s cho mốc nhấn ngắn khi thẻ SPONSORS hiện. | Caption đi theo từng từ có thể để lời dẫn nhịp. Nếu cùng lúc có chữ, ảnh và thẻ phụ, ưu tiên yếu tố mang thông tin chính. |

| **Montage, nhảy, động tác khớp nhạc** | Lấy kick, snare hoặc nhát nhạc có sẵn để đỡ bước chân, pose, cú xoay hoặc đổi shot. Nếu hình cần nhấn chất liệu mà nhạc chưa thể hiện, thêm một lớp foley gọn vào đúng động tác. | **Khung tối, fade, quay vòng về đầu clip hoặc giữ khoảng thở** | Thử hạ/ngắt nhạc theo hình hoặc giữ một nền môi trường thật (`room tone`, `water ambience`, `forest ambience`). Giữ được khoảng hẫng này có thể tạo nhịp rõ hơn việc thêm một cú nổ ở mép chuyển. |

Khi chồng tiếng, mỗi lớp cần lý do riêng trên hình. Ví dụ camera quét qua và vật chạm bàn là hai pha, có thể dùng whoosh cho pha đi và tiếng va đúng chất liệu cho pha chạm. Nếu chữ đã vào trên đúng nhát snare, thử nghe nhát đó trước khi thêm pop. Chọn độ dài theo chuyển động và kiểm lại cùng lời, nhạc, cảnh kế.

## Study a reference video

`scripts/cue_probe.py` measures the picture and the mix on any machine with ffmpeg, numpy and Pillow; `python scripts/cue_probe.py -h` lists its five commands and their arguments. It reports where picture and energy change. Naming the sound takes a listening tool or ears: Earshot, the listening tool of the nine-Reel study, is on the edit machine only, in the venv at `<local path>`, and the study's evidence is in `<local path> Reel SFX Study/6. AI/work`; neither is in the shared vault. On a machine without Earshot, say so and report the `cue_probe.py` and frame findings only.

For a first overview of a whole reference video, `rea-video` (Apache-2.0, the edit machine only: `<local path>`, set `PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8`) is installed since 2026-11-16: `rea-video decompose <video> -o <dir> --lang en`, then `rea-video summary <dir>/breakdown.json`. On a 71 s TikTok unboxing it gave a usable shot list with contact sheets, burned-in subtitles and text overlays as `packaging/subtitles.srt` / `overlays.ass`, a music BPM and SFX that land on text pops. Without a VLM its live-action/AI verdict was wrong (a hand-held phone shot labelled AI) and 63/68 shots were low confidence, so look at the shot sheets yourself and treat its labels as leads. Its speech recognition has no Vietnamese; use Whisper for words. Confirm cue times with `cue_probe.py` before anchoring a sound.

- Survey the entire A/V duration first: `cue_probe.py onsets` and `envelope` for the sound, `cuts` and sparse frames for the picture.
- For fast cuts, flashes, whips and graphic entrances, run `cue_probe.py frames --at <times>`: it lays out every native-rate frame around each cue with its source timestamp. Identify the last outgoing frame, first transformed frame, first readable incoming frame and settling point. Compare the measured audio anchor to the picture phase it supports. If many cues are unknown, take the candidates from `cuts` and confirm each on a `frames` sheet. Describe any gaps in review coverage.
- For a detailed sound study, cover the entire stereo mix in contiguous short Earshot windows with a little lead and tail. Use the timeline to pick cue moments, then use focused audio windows for unclear attacks and decays.
- Write a cue as sound character and envelope → picture phase and offset → likely function → remaining source uncertainty.
- Check attribution in context: compare neighboring words and repeated beats with the cue. A repeating waveform at the music pulse supports a rhythmic accent. Narrow-band harmonics that follow a voiced syllable weaken a standalone-effect claim. `onsets` lists mid and side apart; add a spectrogram when helpful. Treat these signals as evidence from the mix and mark masked or mixed cues as uncertain.
- Describe level drops and texture changes along with hits, including when they coincide with a reveal or loop seam; `envelope` lists the drops. Keep the before/after audio around the action so a quiet gap or tail remains audible.
- Study Reel audio for timing and texture, and choose licensed equivalents for the job.

## Check a new mix

- Compare important cues with their effects muted and at one plausible earlier or later position. Choose the placement that leads the action, conveys the landing, or makes the information legible. Review at normal speed across the complete phrase, including dialogue and the effect tail. Check mono and stereo per [music.md](music.md). Give Oxi the actual review timestamps.
- To audition one cue outside Premiere, run `cue_probe.py ab` on the review export and the SFX file: it writes the stretch without the cue, with the cue's anchor on the target, and with `--shift` the cue earlier or later, all at the same headroom. When the cue sits near a quiet gap or a loop seam, run `envelope` on both files to see whether the cue fills the gap.
- Cut one hit per cue, measure its level against the voice, and log its source and licence, as the Sound pass in [music.md](music.md) describes.
