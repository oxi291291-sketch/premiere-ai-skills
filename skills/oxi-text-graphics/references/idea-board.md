## Six moves, and whether they fit this stage

| Move | Pins | In scope here? |
|---|---|---|
| **Keyword accent**: the line in one colour, exactly one word in an accent colour or bigger | 5, 6, 9 (and 3) | Yes: template colour/size controls. Same idea as the main + secondary pair in `SKILL.md`. Accent colour still passes the contrast check |
| **Font pair**: small sans or light italic next to a big serif or bold italic | 7, 8 | Only the hierarchy (small run-in + big main line): fonts are Oxi's choice |
| **Size by weight**: the punch word huge, the run-in small | 1, 3, 9 | Yes: matches "size follows length" |
| **Glow on dark** | 5, 9 | Only if the template already has a glow or the shot is dark enough; no custom effect |
| **One word per beat, full frame, background flips** | 1, 2, 3 | No for overlay text. Fits a hook, a closing line or a section break, and only when Oxi asks; it is effects work (`oxi-premiere-workflow/references/effects.md`) |
| **Prop or emoji that is the word**; typewriter; scattering letters | 5, 8, 9 | No: effects/element stage, on Oxi's request |

## Talking-head text (Oxi, 2026-11-12)

Oxi pointed to the Pinterest search "talking head"
(https://www.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=talking%20head) as a look that fits many clips.
Read from 16 pin covers on 2026-11-12 (12 relevant; the rest were band art or ads). This
is the closest reference to Oxi's own presenter videos and fits the text stage as it is:

- **Two tiers on the speaker**: a small plain lead-in line and a big keyword under or
  beside it ("What's the / *30,000ft* view", "help you / **Skip** / through",
  "So you can get a full / *content team*", "I don't have / **time.**"). 9 of 12 covers.
  Same idea as the main + secondary pair in `SKILL.md`.
- **The keyword gets the character**: script or italic serif, warm gradient (gold, orange,
  lime), soft glow or a red caps word; the lead-in stays small, white, plain sans.
- **Position on the body**: chest to lower-middle, over the shirt or the microphone,
  centred or slightly off-centre, never over the face. Matches the lower-third default.
- **A small icon inside the line** now and then (a skip icon after "Skip", a calendar
  next to "years").
- **Picture under it is warm and separated** (dark background, warm key light); the text
  colour is picked against that, in line with the contrast rule.
- One idea per screen: 2–6 words, rarely a full sentence.

What does not carry over: before/after framing, UI frames and floating phone cards in
the covers are promo graphics for editors, not part of the edited video.

## Traps seen in these references

- Most pins are agency ads where text is the whole picture. On Oxi's booking videos the
  client's product and the speaker come first: borrow the colour, pairing and size ideas,
  not the full-screen text.
